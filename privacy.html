DeskBoard privacy policy

DeskBoard is a personal desktop app that runs only on its owner's Windows PC.

What it accesses

When you sign in with Google, DeskBoard requests read-only access to your Google Calendar events (calendar.readonly), your Google Tasks (tasks.readonly) and your email address (to label the account). It never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Google account.

Where data goes

Calendar events and tasks are downloaded directly from Google's APIs to your PC and shown on your desktop. They are cached only on that PC. Sign-in tokens are encrypted with Windows (DPAPI) and stored only on that PC. Nothing is sent to any other server, and no data is shared, sold or used for advertising.

Removing access

Remove an account inside DeskBoard (this also revokes its token), or revoke access at any time from your Google Account under Security > Third-party apps.
